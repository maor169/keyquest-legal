Your privacy

Your camera stays with you.

This policy explains the camera and local storage used by MoveMash, optional support messages, and this website.

1. Who we are

MoveMash is a motion arcade developed by Maor Machani under the KeyQuest Studio brand. For app support or privacy questions, email shampory@gmail.com.

This policy covers the first release being prepared for distribution and the MoveMash support website. It does not describe future features that have not been enabled.

2. Camera and movement

Camera play uses the device’s front camera, with your permission, to recognize hands and body movements. Camera frames and body landmarks are processed temporarily in the device’s memory to control the game.

The game does not record, save or upload camera images or body landmarks.

It does not use the microphone or identify a person from their face or movements.

You can choose touch practice without granting camera access. Leaving a camera game with its Back control, or moving the game to the background, closes its camera stream. You can also revoke permission in device or browser settings.

Recognition components are included in installed apps. In a web edition, game files and recognition components are downloaded from the game’s hosting site and may be cached by the browser. Camera processing still happens on your device.

3. Local progress and settings

The game uses local app or browser storage for best scores, stars, medals, progress and preferences such as language, sound and control settings. This data is used to remember your choices and show your progress on that device.

MoveMash does not upload this data to an app server or synchronize it through a MoveMash cloud account. Device backup and restore behavior may depend on the operating system and your device settings; it is separate from MoveMash cloud synchronization.

Local data stays in the app or browser until it is removed through device or browser controls. Clear the game’s site data for browser play, or remove the app and its local data for an installed app. Deleting local data can remove progress and preferences.

4. Accounts, ads and online features

The first release has no MoveMash account sign-in, advertisements, usage analytics, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Game Center and Google Play Games are disabled in this release: MoveMash does not sign in to those services or publish scores to them.

App stores, operating systems and browsers may handle their own service, purchase, diagnostic or backup information under their separate settings and policies. This is distinct from data sent by MoveMash.

5. Messages you choose to send

If you contact shampory@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your request and investigate the problem you describe.

Support email is handled using Gmail. Google processes email through its service under the Google Privacy Policy. Contact us if you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of your support correspondence.

No camera image is automatically attached to a support message. You do not need to send a camera recording to request help. Please do not send passwords, payment information or photos of children. If a child needs help, a parent or guardian should contact us.

6. Visiting this website

These support and privacy pages do not run analytics or advertising scripts, set cookies, use browser storage, request camera access, or include a data collection form. An email link opens the email app you choose; it does not send a message automatically.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, and its hosting service can process ordinary web request information. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you play a web edition of the game, its hosting provider also receives ordinary requests for game files. Those requests do not include camera frames, body landmarks or locally saved game scores.

7. Your choices

  • Play with touch practice if you prefer not to use the camera.
  • Allow or revoke camera access through your device or browser.
  • Remove locally saved game data using app or site storage controls.
  • Choose whether to email support and which details to include.
  • Contact shampory@gmail.com with privacy questions or a request concerning support correspondence.

8. Policy changes

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current policy text. New online or monetization features will need an updated explanation before they are enabled.
