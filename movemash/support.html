MoveMash support

A hand getting started.

Set up camera play, try touch controls, or get help from KeyQuest Studio.

MoveMash is preparing its first store release.

Start playing

  1. Pick a game. Read the instructions on its start screen.
  2. Choose your controls. Use the camera option for movement play, or touch practice for direct screen controls.
  3. Follow the game’s prompt. Camera play may need a short moment to find your hands or body before the round starts.
  4. Return when you need to. Use Back or the menu control to leave a game and choose another.

Common questions

How should I position the camera?

Rest the device on a stable surface with the front camera facing you. Use an evenly lit space and keep the hands or body parts needed by the game visible. Leave enough clear space to move comfortably; pause and reposition the device if reaching starts to feel uncomfortable.

The camera does not open. What should I check?

Allow camera access for MoveMash in your device settings. For browser play, check the browser’s site camera permission and use an HTTPS game address. A downloaded HTML file opened directly from the file system cannot provide the same camera access. Close another app that may be using the camera, then reopen the game. Touch practice is available without a camera.

Why is movement tracking slow or uneven?

Try brighter, even lighting and keep your hands in view. Place the device steadily, close other demanding apps, and let it cool down if it is warm. Rapid movements, a dark room, or hands hidden behind other objects can make tracking less reliable.

How do I play without a camera?

Choose the touch practice option on the game’s start screen. Tap, drag or swipe as the game’s instructions describe. Touch practice can have different reward rules from camera play; the result screen tells you whether progress was saved.

I cannot hear music or effects.

Start a game with a tap, check its sound or mute control, and increase your device’s media volume. Check connected headphones and, on Apple devices, the silent setting. A browser may wait for your first tap before allowing sound.

Can I change the language?

Use the globe control in the app. MoveMash supports English, Hebrew, Arabic, Spanish, French, German and Russian. It starts with a supported device language, falls back to English, and remembers your selection locally.

Where are my scores and preferences saved?

Best scores, rewards and settings are saved locally on the device or in the browser used to play. They do not sync through a MoveMash account. Private browsing, blocked storage, clearing site data or removing app data can prevent saving or remove progress. Practice results are not always saved as rewards.

How do I stop the camera or take a break?

Leave the camera game with its Back control. The game also closes its camera when it moves to the background. When you return, reopen camera play. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device or browser settings.
