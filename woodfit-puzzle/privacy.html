Privacy Policy — WoodFit Puzzle

Last updated: 1 August 2026

WoodFit Puzzle does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no user account. Everything the game saves stays on your own device. The Android version connects to Google Play only to process the optional one-time purchase, and nothing else.

Information we collect

None. WoodFit Puzzle does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information — no name, no email address, no account, no contacts, no location, no device identifiers, and no usage analytics.

What the game stores on your device

So that your progress survives closing the game, WoodFit Puzzle saves a small amount of game state locally. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It consists entirely of:

  • Which puzzles you have completed, and the star rating earned for each
  • The puzzle you have in progress, so you can resume it
  • Your settings: music track and mute state, table skin, vibration, camera controls, table decorations, and whether you have seen the tutorial
  • Total time spent solving, used only for the in-game statistics screen

On Windows this is kept in the standard application settings store; on Android it is kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Network access and purchases

Windows: the game is free, complete, and makes no network connections at all.

Android: the game is free to play, with an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the remaining puzzles. The only reason the app requests internet access is to let Google Play handle that purchase and to confirm, when you reinstall or change device, that you already own it.

That transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name. Google tells the app one thing: whether this account owns the unlock. How Google handles the payment itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Nothing else is ever sent anywhere. Your progress, stars and settings are never uploaded.

Advertising and tracking

There is none. WoodFit Puzzle contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no subscriptions. The only in-app purchase is the single permanent unlock described above.

Children

WoodFit Puzzle is rated suitable for all ages and is safe for children. As it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever adds a feature that requires handling data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
