Quick start

How to play

  1. Drag each car forward or backward along its lane.
  2. Cars cannot turn or pass through one another, so move the blocking cars out of the way.
  3. Open a clear route to the exit on the right.
  4. Slide the marked yellow car out to finish the puzzle.

Undo retraces a move. Hint highlights a useful next move. The first-play lesson can be replayed from Settings → Guided practice.

Progress and devices

Laneway Escape works offline and has no account or cloud sync. Progress is saved on the device. Moving to a new device or removing the app may mean that local progress does not transfer.
