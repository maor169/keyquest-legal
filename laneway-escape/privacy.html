Your privacy

Progress stays with you.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Laneway Escape does not ask you to create an account and does not collect or send personal data from the game. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. The game has no advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

1. Who we are

Laneway Escape is an offline sliding-car puzzle made by KeyQuest Studio. This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. For privacy questions, contact keyqueststudio1@gmail.com.

2. Information the game collects

The current game does not collect, transmit or share personal information. It does not include an account, advertising, analytics, tracking, a social feature or an in-app purchase. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Apple, Google and your operating system provide their own store, device, diagnostic and backup services. Those services may process information under their own settings and privacy policies; they are separate from data collected by Laneway Escape.

3. Progress and settings saved on your device

The game stores local game data so you can continue playing. This includes your current puzzle and moves, completed puzzles and stars, selected car colour and language, and sound or weather preferences. The game does not send this data to a KeyQuest Studio server or synchronize it through a game account.

Your operating system may include app data in a device backup if you have enabled that service. Such a backup is controlled by your device and account settings and is not accessible to KeyQuest Studio. Removing the app may remove its local data.

4. Messages you choose to send

If you email support, we receive the address, message and any attachments you choose to include. Email is handled by Gmail and may be processed under Google's Privacy Policy. Please do not send passwords, payment details or information that is not needed to explain your issue.

5. This website

These pages do not run analytics or advertising scripts, set cookies, use browser storage or include a contact form. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log ordinary web requests, including IP addresses, for its hosting and security services. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children

The game has no social features or personal-data collection. If a parent or guardian has a question about the game or this policy, they can contact us at the address below.

7. Policy updates

If a future version changes how it handles data, this page will be updated to describe that change. The date above identifies the current text.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email keyqueststudio1@gmail.com.
