Your privacy

Your campaign stays on your device.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dark Rift: Warhost does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data. Campaign checkpoints, inventory, and audio preferences are stored strictly locally on your device. There are no ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, or external servers.

1. Who we are

Dark Rift: Warhost is an offline isometric dark-fantasy action-strategy RPG made by KeyQuest Studio. This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. For privacy questions, contact keyqueststudio1@gmail.com.

2. Information the game collects

Dark Rift: Warhost does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It includes no user accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, social networks, or external gameplay servers. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Apple and your operating system provide their own App Store, device, diagnostic, and backup services governed by their own settings and privacy policies.

3. Local save data

The game stores local checkpoint files and preferences on your device so you can continue your campaign (unlocked worlds, seals, party upgrades, equipment, and volume settings). KeyQuest Studio has no access to these local files. Deleting the app may remove its local save data.

4. Support emails

If you email support, we receive your address and message to help resolve your issue. Please do not include passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary personal information.

5. This website

These static pages run no scripts, analytics, or cookies. Hosted on GitHub Pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, email keyqueststudio1@gmail.com.
