Controls

Quick start

  • iPhone & iPad: Tap or drag anywhere on the battlefield to move your hero; nearby enemies are attacked automatically. Hold Attack for ranged weapons and tap skills (Dodge, Nova, Cyclone, Spear) and Send Units to command your companions.
  • Mac: Use WASD/Arrow keys or mouse click to move, keys 1–3 / Space / Attack for skills, M for Command Map, and B for Pack.
