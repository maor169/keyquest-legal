Tactical Guide

Gameplay & Controls

  • Movement & Aim: On iPhone/iPad, use the left on-screen joystick to move and swipe the right side to aim. On Mac/PC, use WASD to move and mouse/trackpad to look.
  • Weapons & Armory: Tap the weapon card or use number keys to switch between your Assault Rifle, Shotgun, Micro Uzi, M203 Grenade Launcher, and RPG-7.
  • Rescue Mission: Locate trapped civilians in the infected city, approach them to initiate rescue escort, and guide them safely to the helicopter extraction zone.
  • Co-op Multiplayer: Connect to the same local Wi-Fi network to team up in tactical squads or jump into the Quarantine Outpost arena.
